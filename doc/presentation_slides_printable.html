System Framework

Core Contract Layer

  • LogisticPlatform.sol: Main platform contract, manages order process
  • CollateralPool.sol: Collateral pool management, stores pledged ETH and mints LogiToken
  • LogiToken.sol: Platform token, supports locking/releasing mechanism

Interaction Layer

  • Sender: Creates orders, confirms handover, evaluates service
  • Courier: Accepts orders, delivers, confirms delivery
  • Receiver: Confirms receipt
  • Secure and transparent information exchange through Ethereum blockchain
6

Main Process

Order Placement & Acceptance

  1. Order Creation: Sender creates order with details (pickup/dropoff locations, deposit, value)
  2. Order Confirmation: Sender confirms order and selects a courier
  3. Order Acceptance: Courier accepts the order assignment
  4. Delivery Handover: Sender initiates delivery process by marking items as sent
  5. Transportation Begins: Courier confirms receipt of package and starts transport

Delivery & Settlement

  1. Delivery Completion: Courier marks delivery as completed
  2. Delivery Confirmation: Receiver confirms receipt of package
  3. Order Finalization: Sender completes the order after verification
  4. Rating and Evaluation: Sender rates courier service (1-5 stars)
  5. Automatic Distribution: Platform distributes payment (80% courier, 17.5% bonus pool, 2.5% platform)

Token and Deposit Mechanism

LogiToken Design

  • Platform-specific token, 1:1 pegged to ETH
  • Dual management of free and locked states
  • Supports redemption for ETH at any time

Deposit Mechanism
